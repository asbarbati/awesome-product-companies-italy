# Awesome Product Companies in Italy

A community-driven directory of technology and non-technology companies operating in Italy that maintain in-house IT, software engineering, or infrastructure departments. 

This repository aims to support tech professionals across all disciplines in finding direct employment opportunities by explicitly filtering out recruitment agencies and pure body-rental providers.

---

## Inclusion Criteria

To be listed in this repository, a company **must satisfy all of the following rules**:

1. **Direct Employment & Project-Based Work (No Body Rental):**
   * The company hires tech professionals directly for internal products, internal IT departments, software products, or fully managed software projects.
   * Pure **body-rental providers** (staff augmentation without project ownership or internal tech leadership) are strictly excluded.
2. **Physical Office in Italy:**
   * Must have an established legal entity, physical headquarters, or operational office registered in Italy.
3. **No Recruitment Agencies / Intermediaries:**
   * Headhunting firms, recruitment agencies, and third-party staffing platforms are excluded.
4. **Active Career Page or LinkedIn Profile:**
   * Must maintain at least an active Careers Page/Portal or an official LinkedIn company page (preferably both).

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome! If you know a company that meets all inclusion criteria, please help expand the list.

### Contribution Steps:
1. **Fork** the repository.
2. Edit `README.md` to add the company in **alphabetical order** to the directory table.
3. Verify that the company respects all **Inclusion Criteria**.
4. Provide valid links to at least the official **Career Page** or **LinkedIn Profile** (preferably both).
5. Submit a **Pull Request**.
